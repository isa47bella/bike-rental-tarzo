Welcome

Thank you for choosing Arfanta Bike Rental. You are in the heart of the Prosecco Hills of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, a UNESCO World Heritage Site: vineyards, villages, lakes and abbeys all within easy pedalling distance. In this guide you will find our recommended routes and the best places to stop for a glass of wine and a good meal.

Our routes

Three loop routes through hills, vineyards and lakes, for every level of fitness. Each route starts a few kilometres from the shop: we will show you how to reach the starting point.

Rented a city e-bike? The routes in this guide are designed for mountain bike e-bikes. Just ask in the shop: we will gladly suggest a more relaxed ride suited to your bike.

Difficulty key

  • Easy   little climbing, suitable for everyone
  • Moderate   some climbs, a bit of fitness needed
  • Challenging   steep climbing, for trained riders
Route data comes from official GPS tracks (Wikiloc).
Easy e-MTB

Follina – Laghi di Revine

Distance 27.7 km
Ascent +263 m
Duration ≈ 2 hrs
Type Loop

A gentle loop linking Follina — famous for its Cistercian abbey — with the still waters of the Revine Lakes. A varied ride through villages, meadows and hills, with little climbing: the right route to enjoy the scenery without effort.

Start: about 6.4 km from the shop, in the Follina area. Scan the QR to follow the route on your phone, with a map and live GPS position.

Moderate e-MTB

Refrontolo – Val Trippera

Distance 33.5 km
Ascent +753 m
Duration ≈ 3 hrs
Type Loop

A loop through the hills of Refrontolo, home to the Molinetto della Croda, an old watermill among the most photographed in the Veneto. Scenic ups and downs through the Prosecco vineyards, with a few more demanding stretches.

Start: about 3.5 km from the shop, in the Refrontolo area. Scan the QR to follow the route on your phone, with a map and live GPS position.
2 / 3 · Refrontolo – Val Trippera
Challenging e-MTB

Pian de le Femene – Col dei Gai

Distance 30.4 km
Ascent +1,133 m
Duration ≈ 3.5 hrs
Type Loop

The scenic climb par excellence: from Tarzo you climb up to Pian de le Femene, a wide plateau at 1,250 m with an extraordinary view over the lakes and the Veneto plain. A challenging route, the reward for those with strong legs.

Start: about 2.9 km from the shop, in the Tarzo area. Scan the QR to follow the route on your phone, with a map and live GPS position.
Please note: steep climbing — set off with the battery fully charged.
